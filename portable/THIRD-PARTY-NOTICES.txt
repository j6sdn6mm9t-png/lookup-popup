DICTIONARY DATA (data/dict.db)
------------------------------
data/dict.db is a database compiled from the sources below. Because it includes material from
Wiktionary, the database is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International License (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
It is rebuilt from the original sources by build_db.py in the project repository.

  English definitions, pronunciations, etymologies:
    Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org/), by Wiktionary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0 / GFDL,
    extracted with wiktextract via kaikki.org (https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/).

  Thesaurus and fallback English definitions:
    Open English WordNet 2024 (https://en-word.net/), CC BY 4.0.

  English–Chinese entries:
    ECDICT (https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), by skywind3000, MIT License.
